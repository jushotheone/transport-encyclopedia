# 10-minute playtest with Zion (and a friend if possible)

Open the live site on the device he would really use (phone or tablet). Do **not** explain anything. Just say:
"This is your explorer game. Have a go." Then watch quietly. Use a timer.

## What to watch (tick or jot a few words)

| Minute | Question | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | Did he name his explorer and press **I'm ready** without help? | |
| 1-2 | Did he understand the map? What did he tap first? | |
| 2-5 | Did he start a **Route Builder** puzzle unprompted? Did he understand "turn tiles"? | |
| | Did the **Route Breaker** break moment get a reaction (laugh, groan, "hey!")? | |
| 5-7 | Did he open a vehicle deck, or an **Expedition**? Which one held his attention longer? | |
| 7-9 | Did he try **Design Studio** or **Fact or Fake**? | |
| 9-10 | When you said "that's enough", did he want to keep going? | |

## Questions to ask afterwards (one at a time, no leading)

1. What was the best bit?
2. What was boring or confusing?
3. Would you play it again tomorrow? Why or why not?
4. What would you add if you were the boss of Zion Transport System?
5. Score it out of 10 (ask him, not you).

## Send back to Claude

- The three things he **skipped or got stuck on**.
- The one thing he **asked to do again**.
- His score, and yours.
- Anything he said that made you smile. That is usually the best feature idea.

Everything he does is saved in the browser on that device only, so you can also open **Passport** afterwards to see
his rank, streak and what he learned.
